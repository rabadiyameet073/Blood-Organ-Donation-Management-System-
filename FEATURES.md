## 🎯 Complete Feature List

### 🗄️ Database Layer (Advanced)

#### **15 Normalized Tables**
- ✅ Third Normal Form (3NF) compliance
- ✅ Referential integrity with cascading deletes
- ✅ Strategic indexing for performance
- ✅ Computed columns for auto-calculations
- ✅ Unique constraints and check constraints

#### **10 Stored Procedures & Functions**
1. **check_blood_compatibility()** - Smart blood type matching
2. **calculate_organ_match_score()** - Multi-factor scoring algorithm
3. **find_best_organ_match()** - Priority-based recipient selection
4. **check_donor_eligibility()** - Automated health validation
5. **update_blood_inventory()** - Thread-safe inventory management
6. **get_low_inventory_alerts()** - Threshold monitoring
7. **register_blood_donation()** - Complete donation workflow
8. **get_dashboard_stats()** - Real-time aggregations
9. **search_donors()** - Advanced multi-criteria search
10. **schedule_appointment()** - Automated scheduling

#### **10 Database Triggers** (NEW! ⭐)
1. **Auto-update inventory** after blood donations
2. **Compliance logging** for all donations
3. **Low inventory notifications** when below threshold
4. **Donor eligibility alerts** after 56-day waiting period
5. **Organ allocation logging** for audit trail
6. **Auto-update transplant requests** on organ allocation
7. **Expired donation cleanup** (scheduled daily)
8. **Appointment reminders** (24 hours before)
9. **Login activity tracking** for security
10. **Patient deletion protection** if pending requests exist

#### **Automated Events**
- Daily expiry cleanup
- Automated reminder system
- Scheduled maintenance tasks
